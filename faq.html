For most adults and children, an oral examination and professional hygiene clean every six months provides dependable preventative protection. This allows your clinician to detect subtle signs of enamel wear, early decay, or gum changes before they develop into discomfort. Depending on your personal gum health and plaque accumulation, a custom frequency between 3 to 12 months may be suggested.

Professional whitening uses clinically tested carbamide or hydrogen peroxide gels that gently penetrate enamel micropores to break down organic stain molecules caused by tea, coffee, and daily foods. In-clinic sessions feature protective barrier gels for your gums, while custom take-home trays offer controlled, gradual brightening with sensitivity-management desensitizing agents.

Traditional braces rely on fixed brackets and gentle wires to guide teeth into position, offering comprehensive control across complex bite misalignments. Clear aligners use a sequence of custom-moulded, transparent thermoplastic trays that can be removed for eating and flossing. Both achieve balanced orthodontic outcomes; our orthodontist will review your teeth and lifestyle to recommend the best match.

We generally advise bringing your child for their initial introduction around their first birthday or within six months of their first primary tooth erupting. This early visit is brief and joyful: we count teeth, allow the child to ride in the chair, and give parents practical guidance on infant brushing, feeding habits, and teething comfort.

If a tooth is knocked out, handle it only by the crown (do not touch the root), gently rinse off visible dirt with milk or saline without scrubbing, and if possible place it back into the socket or store it in cold milk before seeking immediate clinical care. For severe acute toothache, rinse with warm saltwater and contact our practice helpline right away.

Dental anxiety is very common, and you are never alone in feeling nervous. Our team prioritizes patient peace above all else. When you make your appointment, simply let us know. We plan extra appointment time, explain every tool before use, agree upon a simple stop-signal (like raising your left hand), and take breaks as often as you request.

Please bring an accepted form of identification, an up-to-date summary of any medications or allergies, and any recent dental radiographs from previous clinics if available. Arriving 10 minutes prior to your scheduled time allows you to settle into our reception lounge and complete your registration details without rushing.

You can submit an appointment request through our online booking page anytime, or call our front desk during clinic hours at 0000 000 0000. If you need to reschedule or cancel, please provide at least 24 hours advance notice so we may offer that appointment time to other patients.
