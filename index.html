Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to a few common questions patients ask before their first visit.

Most people benefit from a routine dental check-up and professional hygiene clean every six months. However, depending on your individual oral health profile, gum conditions, and dental history, our clinician may recommend an interval tailored specifically to your needs.

Dental anxiety is very common, and our team is trained to provide gentle, empathetic care. We invite you to inform us when booking. We will explain every step beforehand, use stop-signals so you stay in control, and proceed strictly at a pace that feels safe and comfortable for you.

Please bring photo identification, a list of current medications you take, and any previous dental records or recent radiographs if easily accessible. Arriving 10 minutes early allows you to settle into our reception lounge and complete your health history form comfortably.
